Privacy Policy — Checkers Racing (Shashki po gorodu SSSR)

Effective October 6, 2026

1. Data kept only on your device

Your progress and settings: in-game money (₽), owned cars and their paint, track records, experience and level, language, sound, vibration and graphics quality. This data never leaves your device and is deleted together with the game.

2. Online mode (only after you agree)

The first time you open Multiplayer the game asks for your consent. If you agree, the game server (Supabase) stores:

  • an anonymous account identifier — no email, phone number or password;
  • the racer name you entered (other players can see it);
  • your ELO rating, wins and losses;
  • recordings of your online runs: car path, time, car and paint.

Why: so other players can race against your runs and to keep the leaderboard. Other players can see your racer name and rating.

Retention: until you delete your online profile; only your 20 latest runs are kept.

How to delete: Settings › Delete online profile. The profile, runs and anonymous account are removed from the server immediately.

Name reports: you can report another player's name in the leaderboard or after an online race — the player is hidden for you right away, and the server stores who reported whom. After three reports the name is reset to “Гонщик XXXX”. Offensive names are rejected by the game and the server.

3. Ads

The game shows Yandex ads (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK by Yandex LLC). According to the SDK's privacy manifest (version 8.6.0), it collects a device identifier and advertising data to serve ads; Yandex states it does not use them for tracking and does not link them to your identity.

The game does not ask for tracking permission and does not receive Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). The game itself shares nothing about you with Yandex: no age, interests, location, racer name or race data. How ads are chosen: Settings › Ads › How ads are chosen.

How Yandex processes data: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

To report an ad: Settings › Ads › Report an ad (email to Rosonlineapp@gmail.com).

The “No ads” purchase removes ads between races.

4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Only Apple receives payment details; the game only learns which item was bought.

5. Third parties

Data is received only by the services named above: Supabase (online mode server), Yandex LLC (ads) and Apple (purchases). Each of them provides the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy and required by the App Store Review Guidelines. Data is not sold.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

You can withdraw consent for online mode and delete your online data at any time as described above. For other privacy questions, email Rosonlineapp@gmail.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the game and on the game's page.
