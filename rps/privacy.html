Rock Paper Scissors Online — Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026. Developer and data controller: Studio 45 (Dmitry Sakal), email: rpsonlineapp@gmail.com.

1. Stored only on your device

Settings (sound, music, vibration, hints), bot game statistics and a profile cache. Deleted together with the app.

2. Account and online play

On first launch the game creates an anonymous account on the game server (Supabase, Frankfurt data centre, EU) — no email, phone or password. The server stores:

  • an anonymous account identifier;
  • the nickname you chose and your character (visible to other players);
  • ELO rating, wins, losses, draws, streaks and wins against bots (to unlock characters);
  • online games: opponent, moves and battles, result and rating change;
  • language and app version;
  • reports you sent or received;
  • verified purchases (Apple product and transaction identifiers).

Why: to match opponents, run games and the leaderboard, unlock characters and restore purchases. Kept until you delete your account.

Sign in with Apple (optional, in Profile) links your account to your Apple ID so you can restore it on a new device. The game requests neither your name nor your email — the server only receives an Apple user identifier.

Nickname reports: nicknames can be reported from the leaderboard. After three reports from different players a nickname is reset to “Player XXXX”. Offensive nicknames are rejected.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, our server (Supabase) stores your device push token linked to your account identifier and sends notifications through the Apple Push Notification service. It is used for friend invitations, “your turn” reminders and rematch offers.

4. Advertising

The game shows no ads. It does not request tracking permission and does not access Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

5. Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) receive pseudonymous game events (e.g. game started/finished, duration) and crash reports so we can fix bugs and improve the game. Your nickname and moves are not sent there.

6. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Only Apple receives payment details; the game and server only learn which item was bought and verify Apple's signature.

7. Who receives data

Only the services named above: Supabase (server), Google (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics), Apple (purchases, Sign in with Apple and notification delivery). We do not sell data.

8. Deletion

Settings › Delete account (double confirmation) immediately deletes your profile, nickname, rating, push token, invitations, reports and the anonymous account; in your opponents' game history you remain without a name. Purchases stay with your Apple ID and can be restored in Settings.

9. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to processing, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Spain — AEPD). Contact rpsonlineapp@gmail.com.

11. Changes

A new version will be published on this page with a new date.
