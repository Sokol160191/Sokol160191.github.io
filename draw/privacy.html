Draw Like a Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Developer and data controller: Studio 45 (Dmitry Sakal), Barcelona, Spain. Contact: rosonlineapp@gmail.com.

1. Stored only on your device

Settings (music, sounds, vibration, interface language), whether you finished the tutorial, and a cache of your nickname and character. Deleted together with the app.

2. Account

On first launch the game creates an anonymous guest account on our game server (Supabase, data centre in Ireland, EU) — no email, phone or password is needed. Sign in with Apple is optional (Settings): it links the account to your Apple ID so you can continue on another device. We request neither your name nor your email; the server receives only an Apple user identifier.

3. What the server stores and why

  • Profile: account identifier, nickname with a 4-digit tag, character, optional country, interface language, time of last activity (to close abandoned rooms). Nickname, tag and character are visible to other players.
  • Game data: rating, statistics (matches, wins, draws, guessed words, average guess time, best streak), matches you played (teammates and opponents, words, scores, rating change), training rounds.
  • Drawings: the strokes you draw in a match are sent to your teammate and stored on the server so your teammate can get them after a reconnect, both teams can see them on the results screen and reports can be reviewed. They are deleted after 30 days.
  • Training library: a drawing that was guessed in under 60 seconds and was not reported may be kept in the training library, where other players guess it. Only the strokes, the word and the guess time are kept — no link to your account, nickname or match.
  • Ghost teams: when there are not enough players, two people can play against a “ghost” — a recording of a real team from an earlier match. The recording contains only the moments when words were guessed and the two character pictures; no nicknames and no link to accounts.
  • Blocked players: players you blocked, so they are never matched with you. The game has no friends list.
  • Reports: reports you send or receive (about a drawing, nickname or behaviour) and moderation decisions.
  • Notifications: the current version sends no push notifications and does not ask for permission. If an earlier test version stored your device push token and language, it is not used and is deleted together with your account.
  • App events and error reports: pseudonymous events (app opened, match or training started/finished, room created, invite opened, Apple ID linked) and crash/error reports with app version and OS, stored in our own database to fix bugs and improve the game. Kept for 90 days. We do not use third-party analytics.

Legal bases (GDPR): performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) for the account and matches; our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the game safe (reports, blocks, bans), building the training library and ghost teams, and fixing bugs. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us.

4. Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, does not track you across apps or websites and does not access Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

5. Who receives data

Supabase Inc. (hosting of the game server and database, EU region) and Apple (Sign in with Apple). Other players see your nickname, tag, character, your drawings (teammate during the match; both teams in the results). We do not sell or share data for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete the account. Drawings: 30 days. App events and error reports: 90 days. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account — it is deleted immediately with all data listed above, except the anonymous training-library drawings and ghost-team recordings that have no link to you. Without the app, see delete.html.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 12 and over. There is no text chat — only quick reaction stickers. If you believe a child under 14 has given us personal data without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, restrict or object to processing by writing to rosonlineapp@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

9. Third-party content

Some training drawings come from The Quick, Draw! Dataset by Google LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (normalized and simplified for the game).

10. Changes

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date.
